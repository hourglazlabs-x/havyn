# CO alarm

_Carbon monoxide_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Everyone outside now.** Alarm, or headache, dizziness, nausea, confusion. Don't look for the source.
2. **Call 911 from outside.** Tell them a carbon monoxide alarm went off.
3. **Count everyone, pets too.** Do not go back inside for anyone or anything.
4. **Stay out until responders say it's safe.** Don't restart the generator, stove or heater.
5. **Anyone sick, sleepy or confused?.** Tell 911 it may be carbon monoxide. They need medical care. Can't reach 911? Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.
6. **Not responding, not breathing normally: CPR.** Give breaths if you are trained.

> Generators, grills, camp stoves and cars: never indoors, in a garage, carport or basement, even with doors or windows open. Generators at least 20 ft from doors, windows and vents, exhaust pointed away.
