# CPR child

_Child or baby not responding, not breathing normally_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Tap and shout. Breathing normally?.** Baby: tap the foot. Not responding and not breathing normally means start CPR. Gasping is not normal breathing.
2. **Call 911 on speaker.** Alone with no phone at hand: give about 2 minutes of CPR first, then go call.
3. **Hand position.** Child: heel of one or two hands, center of the chest. Baby under 1: heel of one hand, center of the chest just below the nipple line.
4. **Push fast, the right depth.** 100 to 120 a minute. Child: about 2 in (5 cm). Baby: about 1.5 in (4 cm). Let the chest come all the way back up. (metronome 110/min)
5. **If you can: 30 pushes, then 2 breaths.** Each breath just enough to make the chest rise. Breaths matter for children and babies.
6. **Do not stop.** Keep going until help takes over or they breathe normally. AED: use it, with child pads if it has them.
