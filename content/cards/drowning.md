# Drowning

_Pulled from a pool or water, not breathing normally_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Get them out safely.** Reach or throw something that floats; don't jump in unless you're trained. Shout for help. Call 911 on speaker, or have someone call.
2. **Tap and shout. Breathing normally?.** Look at the chest for up to 10 seconds. Gasping is not normal breathing.
3. **Not breathing normally: 2 rescue breaths first.** Tilt the head back, lift the chin, pinch the nose, seal your mouth over theirs. 1 second each, enough to make the chest rise. Baby: cover the nose and mouth with your mouth. Not trained or can't give breaths? Skip to pushes.
4. **Then CPR: 30 pushes, 2 breaths.** Hard and fast in the center of the chest. Keep going until they breathe or help takes over. If you can't give breaths, push anyway. (metronome 110/min)
5. **AED as soon as one arrives.** Quickly wipe the chest dry, put the pads on, follow its voice. Keep CPR going until it's ready.
6. **Breathing again: on their side, then the ER.** Anyone who needed breaths or CPR after being in the water needs a hospital check, even if they seem fine.

> Child CPR depth and hand position are on the CPR child card.
