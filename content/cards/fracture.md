# Broken bone

_Deformed, can't bear weight, bone showing_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Call 911 for big injuries.** A bad fall or crash, heavy bleeding, bone through the skin, a limb that looks bent, numb or bluish fingers or toes, a possible broken neck, back or hip, or they won't respond.
2. **Stop bleeding first.** Press with a clean cloth. Bone through the skin: cover the wound and don't push the bone back in.
3. **Keep it still.** Don't try to straighten it. Support it in the position you found it.
4. **Help is far away: splint it.** Pad it, then fix it so the joints above and below the break can't move: a board, rolled magazines or a pillow, tied on with strips of cloth. Fingers or toes should stay warm and pink; loosen it if they go numb or blue.
5. **Ice, wrapped in cloth.** Never straight on the skin. 15 to 20 minutes at a time, to ease swelling and pain.
6. **Watch for shock.** Faint, pale, fast shallow breathing: lay them down with the head slightly lower than the body, raise the legs if that doesn't move the injury, and keep them warm.

> Possible broken neck or back (fall from height, crash, diving): don't move them unless they're in danger. Keep the head still and in line with the body until help arrives.
