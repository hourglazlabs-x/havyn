# Heat stroke

_Hot, confused, collapsing_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Spot it.** Body 103°F or higher. Hot, red, dry or damp skin. Fast, strong pulse. Confusion or passing out. No thermometer? Confusion in the heat is enough.
2. **Call 911.** Heat stroke is a medical emergency.
3. **Move them somewhere cooler.** Shade, indoors, a cool car parked outside.
4. **Cool them now.** Cold-water immersion up to the neck is best: 15 minutes, or until they are no longer confused. Otherwise soak them with cold water and fan them, and put ice or cold packs on the neck, armpits and groin.
5. **Give nothing to drink.** CDC's rule for heat stroke.
6. **Keep cooling until they improve or help arrives.** Stops responding, not breathing normally: start CPR.

> Alert and thinking clearly, with heavy sweating, cold clammy skin or cramps? Likely heat exhaustion: see the Heat playbook. Any confusion: treat as heat stroke.
