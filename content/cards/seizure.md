# Seizure

_Shaking, unresponsive_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Ease them to the ground.** Move hard and sharp things away.
2. **Protect the head.** Something soft and flat under the head. Take off glasses. Loosen anything around the neck.
3. **Turn them on their side.** Gently, mouth pointing to the ground, to keep the airway clear.
4. **Time it. Hands off.** Don't hold them down. Put nothing in their mouth.
5. **Call 911 if.** Over 5 minutes. Another seizure soon after. Trouble breathing or waking. Not back to normal. Injured. In water. First seizure. Pregnant or diabetic.
6. **Stay until they are fully awake.** No food or drink until fully alert.

> Don't give rescue breaths during the seizure. Breathing usually restarts on its own afterward.
