# Low blood sugar

_Diabetes: shaky, sweaty, confused_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Passed out, seizing or can't swallow? Nothing by mouth.** Food or drink can choke them. Call 911 now.
2. **Give their glucagon if they have it.** Follow the kit. Turn them on their side; they may throw up. Not awake in 15 minutes: a second dose if you have one.
3. **Awake and can swallow? 15 grams of fast sugar.** Glucose tablets or gel (check the label for 15 g), 1/2 cup of juice or regular soda, or 1 tablespoon of sugar or honey.
4. **Wait 15 minutes, then check again.** Still low, or the meter reads under 70: another 15 grams. No better, or worse: call 911.
5. **Back to normal: eat a meal or snack.** Not mealtime? Protein and carbs, like cheese and crackers.
6. **Never give insulin to someone who is low.** It drives the sugar even lower.
