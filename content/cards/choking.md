# Choking

_Can't breathe, cough or speak_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Ask: are you choking?.** If they can cough hard or talk, let them keep coughing and stay with them.
2. **Can't breathe, cough or speak? Call 911.** Or have someone else call while you act.
3. **5 back blows.** Stand behind and slightly to the side. Lean them forward. Hit firmly between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
4. **5 abdominal thrusts.** Fist just above the belly button, other hand over it. Quick pulls inward and upward.
5. **Repeat 5 and 5.** Keep alternating until the object comes out or they stop responding.
6. **Pregnant or too big to reach around?.** Use 5 chest thrusts instead: hands at the base of the breastbone, quick hard thrusts.
7. **They collapse: start CPR.** Lower them to the ground, make sure 911 is called, and start CPR. If you see the object in the mouth, take it out. Never sweep blindly with a finger.

> Baby under 1 year: face down along your forearm, head lower than chest. 5 back blows, then turn face up: 5 chest thrusts with the heel of one hand. Repeat. Never abdominal thrusts on a baby. Baby stops responding: start child CPR.
