# Bleeding

_Life-threatening bleeding_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Make sure you are safe. Call 911.** Put on gloves if you have them.
2. **Find where the blood comes from.** Open or cut away clothing to see the wound.
3. **Press hard with both hands.** Directly on the wound, with gauze or any clean cloth. Do not let up.
4. **Arm or leg? Tourniquet.** 2 to 3 in above the wound, never on a joint. Tighten until the bleeding stops, even though it hurts.
5. **Neck, armpit or groin, or no tourniquet? Pack it.** Stuff gauze or clean cloth tightly into the wound, then press hard with both hands.
6. **Note the time. Never loosen it.** Write the time on the tourniquet or skin. Still bleeding? Add a second tourniquet above the first.
7. **Keep pressure until help takes over.** Do not lift the packing to check it.

> Chest or belly wound: press on it and get help fast. Don't pack chest or belly wounds.
