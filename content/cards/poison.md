# Poisoning

_Swallowed, breathed in, on skin or in eyes_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Won't wake up, not breathing, or a seizure? Call 911.** Start CPR if they aren't breathing normally.
2. **Everything else: call Poison Control.** 1-800-222-1222, free and open 24/7; it reaches your local poison center. Call right away, before any signs. Keep the container in your hand.
3. **Swallowed: don't make them vomit.** Don't give anything to eat or drink, and no activated charcoal, unless the poison center tells you to.
4. **On the skin: clothes off, rinse 15 to 20 minutes.** Under running water.
5. **In the eyes: rinse 15 to 20 minutes.** Running lukewarm water. Take out contact lenses.
6. **Breathed in: get to fresh air.** Don't breathe the fumes yourself. Generator, grill or heater indoors: open the carbon monoxide card.
7. **Tell them.** Age and weight, what it was and how much, when, how (mouth, skin, eyes, breathing), first aid given, whether they vomited, and where you are.

> Keep medicines and chemicals locked up and in their own containers. Not sure it's a poison? Call anyway: the experts would rather hear from you.
