# Won't wake up

_Unresponsive but breathing normally_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Shout, tap their shoulders, check breathing.** Look at the chest for up to 10 seconds. Gasping or irregular breathing is not normal breathing: call 911 and start CPR.
2. **Breathing normally: call 911.** Put the phone on speaker and stay with them.
3. **Kneel beside them, straighten their legs.** Take off glasses and anything bulky in their pockets.
4. **Near arm out, far hand to cheek.** Put the arm nearest you out at a right angle, elbow bent, palm up. Bring the far arm across the chest and hold the back of that hand against the cheek nearest you.
5. **Far knee up, roll them toward you.** Pull the far knee up with the foot flat on the floor, then pull on that leg to roll them onto their side. Bend the top leg at a right angle so they can't roll over.
6. **Tilt the head back, keep watching.** Lift the chin so the airway stays open and anything in the mouth drains out. Check their breathing until help comes. After 30 minutes, turn them onto the other side.
7. **Breathing stops or turns to gasps: CPR.** Roll them onto their back and start CPR.

> Fall, crash or diving accident (possible neck or back injury)? If they're breathing normally, leave them as they are and keep the head still; move them only if they vomit or you must keep the airway clear. Never leave someone unresponsive on their back.
