# Burns

_Heat, chemical, electrical_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Stop the burning.** Away from the heat. Take off jewelry and clothing near the burn, unless it is stuck.
2. **Cool running water, right away.** Cool, not ice. At least 10 minutes, ideally 20. No running water? Use a clean, cool wet cloth. Large burn or small child: watch that they don't get too cold.
3. **Cover loosely.** Clean, dry, non-stick dressing or clean cloth.
4. **Don't pop blisters.** No butter, oil or toothpaste.
5. **When to call 911.** Deep burns, or larger than 3 in. Face, hands, feet, groin or a major joint. Wraps around an arm, leg, chest or neck. Chemical, electrical or explosion burns. Breathed in smoke, or any trouble breathing. Babies, young children and older adults.

> Chemical burn: protect your hands, brush off dry powder, remove soaked clothing, then rinse with lots of running water for at least 15 minutes. Eye: keep rinsing until help arrives. Electrical burn: make sure the power is off at the source before you touch them. In a blackout it can come back at any moment. Be ready to start CPR.
