# Asthma attack

_Wheezing, can't catch their breath_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **After a sting, food or medicine? Epinephrine first.** Sudden trouble breathing then may be anaphylaxis.
2. **Sit them upright and stay with them.** Keep them calm. Loosen anything tight around the chest or neck.
3. **Help them use their rescue inhaler.** Their own quick-relief inhaler, with a spacer if they have one, as their asthma action plan says. No inhaler? Call 911.
4. **Call 911 for any danger sign.** Lips or nails blue or gray, struggling to breathe (hunched over, ribs showing), can't walk or talk well, very sleepy or confused, or the inhaler isn't helping.
5. **Keep using the inhaler as their plan says, or as 911 tells you.**
6. **Won't wake up and not breathing normally: start CPR.**
