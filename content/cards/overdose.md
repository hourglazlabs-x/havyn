# Opioid overdose

_Won't wake up, slow or no breathing_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Shout their name and rub the breastbone hard.** Signs: won't wake up, slow or no breathing, blue or gray lips, tiny pupils, gurgling.
2. **Call 911 now.** Put it on speaker. Say someone won't wake up and isn't breathing normally.
3. **Give naloxone (Narcan).** Nasal spray: tip in one nostril, press the plunger all the way. Don't wait to be sure it's opioids.
4. **Not breathing normally: start CPR with breaths.** 30 pushes, 2 breaths. Can't give breaths? Keep pushing. (metronome 110/min)
5. **No change in 2 to 3 minutes: give a second dose.** New device, other nostril. Keep up CPR in between.
6. **Breathing again: on their side, stay with them.** Naloxone can wear off before the drugs do, and breathing can stop again. They still need the ER.
