# Stroke

_BE FAST_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **B: Balance.** Sudden loss of balance or coordination?
2. **E: Eyes.** Sudden loss or change of vision?
3. **F: Face.** Ask them to smile. Does one side droop?
4. **A: Arms.** Ask them to raise both arms. Does one drift down?
5. **S: Speech.** Slurred, strange, or unable to speak?
6. **T: Time to call 911.** Any one sign, call now, even if it goes away. Note the time it started or when they were last seen well. Don't drive them yourself unless 911 can't be reached.

> Tell the dispatcher: I think this is a stroke.
