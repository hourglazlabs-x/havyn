# Scorpion sting

_Bark scorpion: pain, tingling, numbness_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Call Poison Control.** 1-800-222-1222, free and open 24/7. Most stings are handled safely at home with their help. Children are at higher risk: call right away.
2. **Call 911 for trouble breathing.** Also chest pain, swelling of the face or throat, or hives.
3. **Serious signs at any age: call 911.** Wild or jerky eye movements, drooling, trouble swallowing or talking, twitching, nonstop crying in a child. Antivenom is given in the hospital.
4. **Wash it and cool it.** Soap and water, then a cool compress, not ice. Rest the arm or leg in a comfortable position.
5. **Kill the scorpion; don't try to catch it.** Knowing the type doesn't change the care.

> Pain, tingling and numbness usually start easing within a few hours.
