# Rattlesnake bite

_Every bite goes to the ER_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Move away from the snake.** Don't try to catch or kill it, and don't touch it even if it looks dead.
2. **Call 911.** Every bite needs the emergency room; antivenom is given there. Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) can help too.
3. **Keep them calm and still.** Walk only if needed to get help or a signal.
4. **Keep the bitten arm or leg still, at heart level or a little lower.**
5. **Take off rings, watches, shoes and tight clothing on that limb.** Swelling can come fast.
6. **Don't: tourniquet, ice, cutting or sucking.** No tight bands, ice or ice water, cutting, suction, alcohol or medicines. They don't help and can make it worse.
