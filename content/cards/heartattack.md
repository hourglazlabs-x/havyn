# Heart attack

_Chest pain, pressure or squeezing_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Chest pain or pressure? Call 911 now.** Don't wait to be sure. It can spread to an arm, the back, neck, jaw or stomach, and come with shortness of breath, a cold sweat, nausea or lightheadedness. Women more often have breathlessness, nausea, back or jaw pain.
2. **Sit them down and keep them still.** Loosen tight clothing. Nobody drives them to the hospital: paramedics start treatment on the way.
3. **Aspirin: chew and swallow.** An alert adult with no aspirin allergy, who hasn't been told by a doctor to avoid it: 162 to 325 mg (one regular 325 mg tablet, or two to four 81 mg low-dose tablets). Not sure? Wait for the paramedics or ask the 911 dispatcher.
4. **Get ready for the paramedics.** Unlock the door. Have their medicines and medicine list ready (Family › their card).
5. **They collapse and aren't breathing normally: CPR.** Start CPR and send someone for an AED if there is one.

> Aspirin never replaces the 911 call. Don't give it to children for chest pain.
