# CPR

_Teen or adult not responding, not breathing normally_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Tap and shout. Breathing normally?.** Not responding and not breathing normally means start CPR. Gasping is not normal breathing.
2. **Call 911 on speaker.** Send someone to find an AED. The dispatcher can coach you.
3. **Hands on the center of the chest.** Heel of one hand, other hand on top. Arms straight, shoulders over your hands.
4. **Push hard and fast.** 100 to 120 a minute, to the beat. At least 2 in (5 cm), not more than 2.4 in (6 cm). Let the chest come all the way back up. (metronome 110/min)
5. **AED here? Turn it on.** Follow its voice. Keep pushing until it tells you to stop.
6. **Do not stop.** Keep going until help takes over or they breathe normally. If someone can take over, switch about every 2 minutes.

> Trained in breaths? 30 pushes, then 2 breaths. Breaths matter most after drowning, carbon monoxide or choking. Child or baby: use the CPR child card.
