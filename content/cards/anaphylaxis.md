# Allergy

_Anaphylaxis: severe allergic reaction_

Checked against sources: 2026-11-14 · Status: Awaiting EMT or nurse review

1. **Spot it.** After a food, sting or medicine: trouble breathing or wheezing, tight throat, swollen lips or tongue, hives all over, repeated vomiting, pale or blue skin, dizziness or fainting. Or mild signs in two body areas at once.
2. **Epinephrine now.** Auto-injector: middle of the outer thigh, through clothing is fine. Nasal spray: into one nostril. Follow the device's own instructions.
3. **Call 911. Say anaphylaxis.** They need the ER even if they start to feel better.
4. **Lay them flat, legs raised.** Trouble breathing: let them sit up. Vomiting: turn them on their side.
5. **No better after 5 minutes?.** Or getting worse: give a second dose with a new device, if you have one.
6. **Stops responding, not breathing normally: CPR.** Start CPR and keep going until help arrives.

> Epinephrine is the first treatment. Don't rely on antihistamines or inhalers instead.
