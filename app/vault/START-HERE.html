HAVYN VAULT

The family's offline library. Made by the HAVYN vault script (1.3.0+4b60c483ec); catalog checked 2026-11-14.

In an emergency: the emergency cards and playbooks live in the HAVYN app on every phone and in the printed binder. This drive is the deep library behind them.

What's in each folder

02-encyclopediasEncyclopediasWikipedia, Wikivoyage, the dictionary and textbooks. Open in Kiwix on a laptop or phone, or Add files in HAVYN.
03-medicalMedicalMedical encyclopedias and drug references. Some are website copies that need Kiwix.

08-softwareSoftwareInstallers for Kiwix, VLC, radios, maps and servers, for Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and Raspberry Pi.
09-builderBuilderProgramming references, if you chose everything.

Open it on an iPhone

  1. Plug the drive in (USB-C iPhones directly; older iPhones need Apple's Lightning to USB 3 adapter).
  2. HAVYN → Library → Add files → Browse → the drive → pick a file in 01-phone-set. HAVYN keeps its own copy.
  3. Big archives and video: open them in the Kiwix app instead (Files → long-press the file → Share → Kiwix).

Share it with every phone (home library)

  • Easiest: a laptop plus the drive — Plug the vault drive into any laptop with Kiwix, open the archives, then turn on its Hotspot (Kiwix calls it that; it shares over your home Wi-Fi). Phones scan the QR code or open the address it shows in Safari. Put the laptop and the Wi-Fi router on the power station: the router keeps working without internet.
  • No laptop: one iPhone or iPad with Kiwix — Kiwix for iPhone, iPad and Mac can share chosen archives the same way (Hotspot, then show the QR code). Load the big archives on the device with the most storage.
  • Always-on: Raspberry Pi 5 with an SSD — A small, low-power server running kiwix-serve from the drive, on the router or its own Wi-Fi. Kiwix Hotspot supports Pi 3B through Pi 500.
  • Everything: a mini PC with Project N.O.M.A.D. — Free, open-source server that adds Khan Academy courses, offline maps and a local AI assistant to Kiwix. Install and download its content while you still have internet.

Keep it current

Run the same script again: it fetches newer versions, resumes unfinished downloads, re-checks checksums, and removes older copies of the same archive. Keep a second drive in sync and store one away from home.
